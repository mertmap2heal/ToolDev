REQ-FCS-2216 Yaw damper minimum gain margin
Performance In review Critical Suspect link 2.2.2 Roll & yaw DAL A
Yaw damper minimum gain margin

The yaw damper shall maintain a gain margin of at least 6 dB across the cruise and approach envelopes when subjected to a single-axis hardover failure of the rudder actuator. Margin to be demonstrated by closed-loop frequency analysis at corner Mach numbers and CG limits.

Upstream REQ-FCS-2200 — Roll/yaw axis stability targets edited 2 d 04 h ago · M. Aldridge
Change stability margin ≥ 4 dB ≥ 6 dB across envelope re-verify needed

Statement

The yaw damper shall achieve a closed-loop gain margin ≥ 6 dB and a phase margin ≥ 45° across cruise (FL280–FL410) and approach (≤ 5,000 ft AGL) flight regimes, with one rudder servo channel held at hardover. Acceptance: Bode analysis at 6 corner points (M, h, CG) plus piloted-sim hardover demo VER-FCS-0418-c.
