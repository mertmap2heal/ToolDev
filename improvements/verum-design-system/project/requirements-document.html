Roll & yaw control laws

This module specifies the closed-loop behavior of the lateral-directional flight control laws under nominal and single-failure conditions. Verification is by analysis at six envelope corners and piloted-simulator demonstration.

12 requirements baseline B-2026-04-12 DAL A owner K. Sato 3 suspect 2 in review

2.2.2.1Stability margins under single failure

The yaw damper and roll-augmentation paths shall maintain stable closed-loop behavior under any single-axis hardover or runaway failure of an actuator channel. Margins are evaluated at the corner conditions defined in §2.2.2.4.

REQ-FCS-2214 Roll rate command saturation at high AoA Functional Critical Released
The roll-rate command path shall be saturated to ≤ 30 °/s when angle of attack exceeds α_stall − 2° for more than 100 ms, to prevent departure into post-stall roll.
Rationale
Prevents excitation of post-stall roll departures observed in wind-tunnel data set WT-22-04. Derived from REQ-FCS-2200 axis-stability targets.
Acceptance
Sim demonstration VER-FCS-0418-c · pass margin ≥ 25 % stick travel headroom.

2.2.2.2Trim, override and pilot interaction

REQ-FCS-2217 Roll axis trim-rate limit Performance High Released
Below 2,000 ft AGL, autopilot roll-trim authority shall be limited to ≤ 4 °/s with a hard upper bound of ±15° commanded bank angle.
Rationale
Bounds the recoverable disturbance the pilot must counter during approach and go-around. Negotiated with human-factors during ARP4754A allocation review.

REQ-FCS-2218 Sideslip envelope hard limit Safety Critical In review
In normal law, sideslip shall be hard-limited to |β| ≤ 8°. Degraded laws may relax to |β| ≤ 14°; alternate law shall annunciate the relaxation to the crew.
